- **Renal adjustment required:** Flagged for drugs with renal clearance dependency (aminoglycosides, cephalosporins, carbapenems, fluoroquinolones, metronidazole, cotrimoxazole, zinc, fluconazole, vancomycin, tinidazole, digoxin, furosemide, levetiracetam, fentanyl, phenytoin).
- **Hepatic adjustment required:** Flagged for drugs with hepatic metabolism (chloramphenicol, erythromycin, nevirapine, primaquine, prednisolone, morphine, pethidine, linezolid, metoclopramide, propofol, fentanyl, phenytoin).
- **Weight bands:** Derived from WHO IMCI consensus (0–<2m, 2–4m, 4–6m, 6–12m, 12–24m, 2–5y, 5–12y, 12–18y) with splitting where drug-specific guidance differs.
- **GAP rows:** Row 113 marks deferred endocrine content (testosterone, androgen replacement in prepubertal children out-of-scope for v1).
- **Out-of-cohort drugs:** Chemotherapy, full neonatal intensive care (e.g. CPAP/SIMV drug protocols), and cardiac surgery–specific drugs excluded from Wave 1.
