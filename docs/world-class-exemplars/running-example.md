# Running Example - Chwezi Cross-Border Trade Digitisation Study

Last verified: 2026-07-08

This is the shared running example for the upgraded engine. It is deliberately framed as a synthetic, evidence-discipline-safe engagement: sample names and scenario details are illustrative, and any real-world claim must be verified before use in a live deliverable.

## Scenario

Chwezi Core Systems is asked to prepare a decision-grade research pack on whether a donor-backed digital trade facilitation programme should support informal cross-border traders in East Africa. The audience is a steering committee that needs to decide whether to fund a pilot and what evidence gaps must be closed first.

## Core research question

Should the programme fund a 12-month pilot of trader-facing digital services at selected East African border points, and what evidence would make that decision defensible?

## Sub-questions

| Workstream | Question | Primary outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Policy and regulation | What rules, agencies, and compliance burdens shape the trader journey? | Regulatory map, source register, risk note |
| User and market reality | What pain points do traders, transporters, border officials, and intermediaries report? | Cohort evidence table, interview guide, findings memo |
| Data and systems | What official datasets, APIs, registries, and platform evidence exist? | Dataset inventory, quality profile, gap register |
| Verification and OSINT | Which claims about border operations, scams, fees, or queues can be verified from open sources? | Provenance log, media audit, claim graph |
| Decision support | What pilot options, risks, assumptions, and trigger metrics should the committee use? | Executive recommendation, tradecraft review, evidence pack |

## Evidence discipline

No live statistic, border name, ministry claim, rate, legal threshold, or named organisation should be asserted from this example unless verified in the current project. The example teaches structure, not facts.

## Deliverable variants

- Executive memo: answer-first recommendation, options, risks, decision criteria.
- Academic/systematic review: PRISMA-informed search log, inclusion/exclusion table, transparent limitations.
- OSINT dossier: source-tiered claims, provenance chains, media-forensics notes.
- Data journalism pack: dataset inventory, cleaning notes, chart-ready findings with caveats.
- Policy brief: regulatory source hierarchy, implementation risks, stakeholder implications.

## Reuse rule

Use this running example for worked examples in sub-skills. Keep its factual claims synthetic unless a live source register is attached.
