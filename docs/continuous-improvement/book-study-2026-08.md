# Book Study and Cross-Engine Improvement Map — August 2026

This is an independent operational synthesis of the sixteen user-provided files. The books remain outside the repositories. The study records applicability, evidence limits, and implementation decisions rather than reproducing source text.

## Intake and evidence limits

| Book | Intake status | Current-year treatment |
|---|---|---|
| *MSC Software Magazine, Volume X — Winter 2019* | Readable magazine and case studies | Useful for case-study structure, simulation thinking, data/model integration, sustainability framing, and engineering proof. Product facts and benchmarks are historical and must not be treated as 2026 platform facts. |
| *Agile Processes in Software Engineering and Extreme Programming — XP 2026* | Readable conference proceedings; explicitly open access | High relevance for 2026 Agile practice: sustained change, value retrospectives, team autonomy, experimentation, architecture uncertainty, AI-augmented engineering, UX pilot testing, and AI in Agile teams. Individual findings remain research claims requiring source-aware use. |
| *Digital Storytelling: A Creator’s Guide to Interactive Entertainment* | Readable, broad historical/practical treatment | Durable principles for interactive narrative, characters, choices, rewards, modular structures, cross-media, audience fit, team documents, and pitching. Technology/platform examples are dated. |
| *AI for Game Developers* | Readable 2005 technical book | Evergreen algorithmic foundations: steering, chasing, evasion, flocking, pathfinding, scripting, finite-state machines, fuzzy/Bayesian methods, neural-network orientation. APIs, hardware assumptions, and production practices require 2026 replacement sources. |
| *Video Game Storytelling* | Readable | Durable shared language for conflict, structure, character, narrative design, and cross-disciplinary collaboration. Examples and platform assumptions should be refreshed. |
| *Platform Enterprise* (Lesley Cordero) | Early release; only Chapters 1–2 available; later chapters unavailable | Relevant 2026 framing for sociotechnical platform engineering, joint optimisation, platform sustainability, team/user needs, organisational structure, and platform-as-product. Do not treat the unfinished manuscript as a complete implementation standard. |
| *Designing for AI* (Arash Sadr) | Early release; Chapters 1–3 available; Chapters 5–19 unavailable | Relevant 2026 design direction: problem-first AI selection, system-centred design, human/AI/system layers, model-vs-system distinction, data/inference transparency, control, oversight, and drift-aware thinking. AI Act and other current legal claims require independent verification. |
| *Leveling Up as a Tech Lead* | Early release; Preface and Chapters 1–3 available; Chapters 4–12 unavailable | Useful for role clarity, growth plans, reflection/adjustment, trust, transparency, communication, stakeholder relationships, and non-blaming learning culture. Treat as partial, not a complete tech-lead curriculum. |
| *LEAN: Ultimate Collection* (2018) | Readable compilation with duplicated sections and uneven source quality | Useful as a practical orientation to Build–Measure–Learn, validated learning, innovation accounting, KPI cadence, Kaizen, DMAIC, Kanban, Scrum, waste/value, and experimentation. Use the original authoritative sources for standards and exact definitions. |
| *The Nonprofit Guide to Strategic Planning* | Readable practical guide | Strong reusable planning architecture: readiness, governance, stakeholders, current-state baseline, external scan, options/trade-offs, resource implications, testing, approval, implementation cadence, KPIs, and plan refresh triggers. Treat publisher templates as guidance, not evidence for a specific nonprofit. |
| *Facility Move Playbook* | Readable practical playbook | Strong transferable operating pattern for complex change: charter, scope, decision rights, continuity, baseline, readiness, inventory, process/capacity, future-state design, risk, cutover, stabilisation, closeout, and lessons learned. |
| *Paid for Your Perspective* | Readable practical expert-network guide | Supports expert positioning, evidence-bounded advisory work, screening/compliance, preparation, call delivery, follow-up, profile design, and a durable knowledge-product pipeline. Compliance boundaries must be retained and jurisdiction-specific claims verified. |
| *Kaizen and the Art of Creative Thinking* (Shigeo Shingo) | Unusable extraction: one-line image-converter notice, no book text | Not admitted for substantive synthesis. Re-ingest a lawful, readable copy before implementation claims are attributed to this title. |
| *Applying the Kaizen in Africa* (Otsuka, Jin, Sonobe et al., 2018) | Readable open-access book | High relevance to the portfolio’s Uganda/East Africa context: 5S, muda elimination, PDCA, participatory and incremental improvement, QC circles, QC Story, standardisation, on-site observation, management commitment, training, and institutional sustainability. Africa cases inform adaptation, not guaranteed results for our practice. |
| *Anatomy for Artists* (Tom Fox) | Unusable extraction: one line (`Part1`) only | Not admitted for substantive synthesis. Re-ingest a complete, lawful copy before adding anatomy-specific guidance. |
| *Dynamic Characters* (3DTotal.com) | Readable tutorial collection | Supports visual development, silhouettes, anatomy-under-design, gesture and story-driven poses, dynamic composition, framing, readability, value separation, and artist-to-modeler handoff. The Photoshop-specific workflow is dated; principles are transferable. |

## Specific improvements to implement

### Skills-web-dev and game development

- Add a Kaizen engine/product audit skill that audits web, mobile, desktop, AI, SaaS, database, DevOps, and game outputs.
- Strengthen `skill-engine-audit` with the 65-cap/95-target contract and a product-output checklist.
- Add a game Kaizen reference: hypothesis-led prototypes, playtest evidence, narrative/gameplay alignment, AI behaviour telemetry, performance/accessibility budgets, standardisation after green evidence, and rollback when an experiment harms player experience.
- Strengthen AI product skills with problem-first AI selection, system maps, model/system/input/output separation, human control, drift monitoring, and post-launch learning loops.
- Strengthen platform/DevOps guidance with sociotechnical joint optimisation, platform-as-product feedback, cognitive-load reduction, and sustainable ownership.

### SRS-skills

- Add a governance skill for engine and product improvement audits.
- Add Kaizen clauses to PRD, game brief, AI PRD, architecture, test strategy, retrospective, release runbook, and evidence-pack outputs: baseline, hypothesis, measure, owner, standardisation, next cycle.
- Add a product-audit evidence matrix covering requirements, traceability, failed-path evidence, accessibility, security, build/release evidence, and user feedback.
- Use Facility Move and Nonprofit Guide patterns for change charters, decision rights, readiness, cutover, stabilisation, and plan refresh.

### Business-plan-skills

- Add a Kaizen/living-plan skill that converts strategic assumptions into a monthly learning backlog and quarterly plan refresh.
- Strengthen market validation with Build–Measure–Learn, validated learning, innovation accounting, and small reversible tests.
- Strengthen management, M&E, and digital-transformation sections with capability maturity, owner/cadence, leading indicators, counter-metrics, and explicit stop/pivot rules.
- Add nonprofit and facility-move variants that expose mission, stakeholder, resource, continuity, and implementation assumptions.

### Proposal-skills

- Add a proposal/product-improvement audit skill with capped evaluator-readiness scoring and a 95-target remediation plan.
- Strengthen proposal storytelling with interactive narrative logic: audience, tension, choice, proof, consequence, and evaluator journey; never substitute story for evidence.
- Strengthen methodology, M&E, change-management, and technical transformation references with PDCA, QC Story, problem/root-cause analysis, standardisation, learning reviews, and beneficiary/user feedback.
- Add expert-positioning and compliance-bounded advisory patterns from *Paid for Your Perspective*.

### Digital-research-skills

- Add Kaizen to research orchestration: after every wave, capture source/claim defects, run a small process experiment, update the standard, and re-measure citation/verification quality.
- Add a research-product audit covering source provenance, claim graph, verification, reasoning, uncertainty, reproducibility, usability, and release evidence.
- Maintain a source status register for early-release, incomplete, historical, and corrupted book inputs; never allow them to masquerade as current standards.

### Website-skills and social-media-skills

- Add product/campaign audits that score audience value, evidence, accessibility, technical/visual quality, conversion or engagement logic, handoff, and learning instrumentation.
- Strengthen website discovery and campaign strategy with problem-first AI, system-level stakeholder mapping, meaningful friction, trust/calibration, and post-launch learning.
- Use Dynamic Characters and Digital Storytelling for visual narrative, character/brand expression, interactive sequencing, and cross-format content—but route visual typography/UI decisions through the design-system engine.

### Design-system-skills

- Add a design-engine/product-improvement audit skill with the 65-cap/95-target contract, evidence matrix, and design-quality-gate handoff.
- Strengthen visual development with readable silhouettes, gesture/story-driven composition, value separation, framing, and artist-to-implementation handoff from *Dynamic Characters*; do not invent anatomy guidance from the unreadable *Anatomy for Artists* extraction.
- Strengthen narrative information architecture, audience empathy, interactive sequencing, and visual critique loops using *Digital Storytelling* and *Video Game Storytelling*.
- Add problem-first AI interface guidance covering system/model/input/output separation, disclosure, user control, correction, contestability, drift, and rollback from *Designing for AI*.
- Apply PDCA, standardisation, evidence capture, and small reversible critique experiments from *Applying the Kaizen in Africa* and *LEAN* to design systems, tokens, components, and visual QA.

### Chwezi accounting doctrine and linux-skills

- Add Kaizen to control and operations governance: baseline the close, reconciliation, incident, provisioning, backup, hardening, and recovery processes; remove muda; standardise verified improvements; preserve segregation of duties and rollback.
- Add finance/operations product audit outputs with evidence, control effectiveness, residual risk, owner, remediation, and re-test.
- Apply Facility Move’s readiness/cutover/stabilisation pattern to ERP, infrastructure, data, and continuity changes.

## What must not be implemented from the books without verification

- 2019 product capabilities, benchmarks, or vendor claims as 2026 facts.
- 2005 game-AI APIs, engine assumptions, or neural-network production guidance as current practice.
- Early-release chapters that are explicitly unavailable.
- The two unreadable extractions.
- Legal, regulatory, platform, tax, market, or safety claims without current source verification.
- Any raw book text or OCR dump in an engine repository.
