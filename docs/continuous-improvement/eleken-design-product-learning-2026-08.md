# Eleken design/product learning record — 2026-08-23

## Purpose and method

This is an internal cross-engine learning record, not a client-facing market or
benchmark report. The supplied Eleken pages were opened on 2026-08-23 and
evaluated as practitioner/agency sources: useful for recurring patterns and
case prompts, but not sufficient authority for standards, market size, product
outcomes, or universal thresholds. The synthesis was checked against W3C WCAG
2.2, Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines onboarding guidance, and GOV.UK Service
Manual guidance on user research and prototyping.

## Standardised synthesis

| Principle | Engine-ready control | Excluded overreach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A design system is a maintained product | Define scope, consumers, owner, contribution states, documentation, release notes, adoption/quality signals, and review cadence. | No universal team size, timeline, or adoption target. |
| First value beats first tour | Name the activation event, measure time-to-value, defer nonessential signup/setup, use contextual teaching, and preserve skip/resume/recovery. | No claim that frictionless signup is always lawful or safe; regulated prerequisites remain explicit. |
| Validation escalates with risk | Start with demand/problem evidence, then interviews/observation, prototype, workflow/concierge, and real behaviour or commitment. Predeclare thresholds and decisions. | No claim that a landing page, survey, or case study proves product-market fit. |
| Trust is demonstrated in the flow | Show material costs, permissions, consequences, status, support/recovery, and reversibility at the decision point. | No invented security, regulatory, or financial assurance. |
| Dashboards are decision surfaces | Name the user and decision, tier metrics, show comparison and freshness, preserve filter context, and design loading/empty/error/stale states and first-run value. | No visual “catchiness” or five-second heuristic treated as a measured universal law. |
| Launch and scale require evidence | Gate launch/scale claims on activation, retention or repeat behaviour, delivery capacity, economics, and a reversible next experiment. | No Eleken market-size, CAC/LTV, growth, funding, or case-outcome figures. |
| AI accelerates execution, not judgement | Preserve the source context, ask for questions/options before generation, record decisions, verify external/product facts, prototype early, and apply scoped deltas with a human reviewer. | No autonomous product decisions, unverified competitor summaries, or time-saved promises. |
| Consistency is behavioural as well as visual | Audit visual, functional, content, layout, and motion patterns; reconcile duplicates through tokens/components, documentation, ownership, and implemented-output QA. | No claim that visual sameness alone improves business outcomes. |
| UX ideas start from observed friction | Use analytics, research, support logs, and observation to identify repeated effort or error before selecting a pattern; measure whether the friction decreases. | No inspiration-gallery copying or feature addition without a user problem. |
