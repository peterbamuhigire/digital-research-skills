# AI-Slop Responsibility Assessment — 11 September 2026

## Decision

Retain the anti-slop gates, but make their primary target publishing responsibility rather than authorship detection. The supplied article is adopted as an editorial standard and evidence-review input. It does not establish validated thresholds for the 25 stylistic observations and must not be used as a scorecard for accusing an individual of AI use.

Canonical cross-engine standard: [`ai-slop-responsible-publishing-standard-2026-09-11.md`](../../../skills-web-dev/references/ai-slop-responsible-publishing-standard-2026-09-11.md).

## Evidence status

- The Kobak record reports an aggregate analysis of more than 15 million biomedical abstracts from 2010–2024 and an estimate of at least 13.5% of 2024 abstracts processed with LLMs. It cannot certify an individual paragraph. The arXiv record shows a 2025 *Science Advances* journal reference.
- The Rallapalli record is a 15 April 2026 arXiv submission. Its abstract reports genre effects stronger than source effects in the studied design, alongside model and decoding effects. It remains a preprint in this review.
- The Liang record reports detector misclassification of non-native English writing in the evaluated systems and samples. It supports caution, not a claim about every detector.
- Google’s link guidance concerns crawlable internal links and site structure; snippet guidance concerns useful page descriptions and generated snippets; generative-AI guidance concerns accuracy, relevance, added value, and scaled content abuse. None is an authorship detector.

## Operational change

1. Ask what the reader loses if a unit disappears.
2. Build a claim ledger before publication: claim, scope, date, source, transformation, limitation, and decision consequence.
3. Label hypothetical examples and never convert generated material into reported experience.
4. Keep style observations, factual defects, provenance failures, and authorship questions in separate fields.
5. Run ME1–ME7 at each major iteration, cite exact evidence, and mark missing inspection as `NOT_ASSESSED`.
6. Require one hard case, counterexample, limitation, or failure path where it could change the recommendation.
7. Prefer deletion or qualification over decorative rewriting when the material has no semantic delta.

## Rejected changes

- No universal banned-word threshold was added.
- No em-dash, bullet-count, heading, name, or meta-description rule was promoted to authorship evidence.
- No detector score is accepted as proof of human or machine authorship.
- No current claim is retained without a dated source record and scope limitation.
