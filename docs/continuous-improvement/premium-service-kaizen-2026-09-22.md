## Anti-slop and value tests

- Substitute a competitor's name: an interchangeable argument needs revision.
- Remove prestige adjectives: the evidence and service should still explain the fee.
- Require actual customer insight or clearly labelled hypotheses; synthetic personas
  and demonstrations cannot become customer research or testimonials.
- Trace numbers, credentials, case studies and claims; remove or qualify unsupported ones.
- Show one real creative/work-product sample before scaling a calendar or narrative.
- Challenge capacity, alternatives, failure/recovery and total cost.
- Separate marketing targets from supplier-controlled commitments and acceptance.
- Match jurisdiction, sector, audience, data, rights and procurement obligations to
  the specific engagement. An index page or international code is not compliance certification.
