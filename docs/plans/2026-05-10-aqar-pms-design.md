# Aqar PMS — Pan-African Product Research: Design (approved)

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Project:** `projects/aqar-pms-design/`
**Approval:** Confirmed by user 2026-05-10 (5 cohorts, design-from-scratch lens, sub-agents not shown the prior prototype)

## Goal

Produce a research dossier that explains why the Aqar PMS prototype looked indistinguishable from Excel to the property mogul, and what genuinely beats Excel for both a $5–$10/month solo landlord and a $1000/month PM firm in Pan-African markets.

## Cohorts (single-wave, parallel dispatch)

1. **Landlords & PM firms** — `landlords-firms/` — workflows, tooling, pain points, WTP across UG/KE/NG/ZA
2. **Excel diagnosis** — `excel-diagnosis/` — why Excel wins, where it breaks, what beats it
3. **Competitive landscape** — `competitive-landscape/` — global + African PMS teardown
4. **Differentiation & magic moments** — `differentiation/` — AI features, two-tier SaaS playbook, regional integrations
5. **Tenant experience** — `tenants/` — renter pain points, payment/receipts/maintenance/deposit recovery

## Discipline clauses (verbatim in every brief)

- Hard-constraint clause from `skills/source-evaluation/references/evidence-discipline.md`
- Wikipedia two-halves clause
- Pricing-evidence clause (year + currency on every quote, ranges with named anchors)

## Synthesis

Orchestrator-only:
1. Triangulate claims across cohorts (≥2 = upgraded confidence)
2. Critical-reasoning pass — warrants, assumptions, countercases
3. Mogul rebuttal — explicit answer to "what does this beat that Excel cannot?"
4. Two-tier feature blueprint
5. Open-gap log for possible Wave 2

## Deliverable

~30–50 page Word report at `projects/aqar-pms-design/report-v1-<date>.docx`

## Out of scope

- Code changes to Aqar
- Primary interviews
- Tech-stack / architecture recommendations
