# Recommended Books and Reading Material

## Must Encode Now

| Title | Author/issuer | Gap filled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Craft of Research | Wayne Booth, Gregory Colomb, Joseph Williams | Question, claim, evidence, warrant, and argument discipline. |
| The Minto Pyramid Principle | Barbara Minto | Executive synthesis and top-down communication. |
| Psychology of Intelligence Analysis | Richards Heuer | Biases, competing hypotheses, and analytic humility. |
| Verification Handbook | Craig Silverman et al. | Digital verification methods for claims, images, and eyewitness material. |
| PRISMA 2020 Statement | PRISMA Group | Systematic-review reporting discipline. |
