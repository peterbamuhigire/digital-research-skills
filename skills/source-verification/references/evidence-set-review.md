# Evidence-set review alongside claim verification

Use this reference to inspect a research packet as a set. It supplements the claim-by-claim source verifier; it cannot promote an unsupported claim or certify factual truth.

## Record

For the stated decision and scope, preserve:

- source IDs, exact locators/snapshots, original order, source-origin/dependency cluster, date, and authority basis;
- claim IDs, their source links and support states, required subquestions, covered subquestions, gaps, and contradictions;
- criteria selected and actually loaded, criterion-bank version, guidance hashes, run/runtime identity, and whether routing labels were visible;
- packet state, unresolved critical items, next action, and which findings remain `NOT_ASSESSED`.

## Check

1. Confirm each material claim still passes the normal source-verification rules, including locator, scope, date, and support state.
2. Group copied/syndicated items by their declared origin cluster. Report cluster counts; do not call a cluster independent corroboration.
3. Compare required subquestions with the claims and evidence that address them. Uncovered questions remain explicit gaps.
4. Preserve conflicting sources and unresolved contradictions. Do not average them away or downgrade one source only because it opposes the preferred conclusion.
5. Check that source authority and method fit the specific claim, and that differing evidence types add distinct value to the decision.
6. Block `ready` when a mandatory control fails, a critical evidence locator is absent, or a material contradiction remains unresolved. Use `NOT_ASSESSED` when required evidence or reviewer judgement is missing.

## Boundary

This process evaluates the documented packet and labels. It does not fetch sources, infer credibility from page count, decide law, reconstruct missing source content, or replace independent review. A schema pass is structural evidence only. Keep the existing source verifier's claim-level release gate unchanged.
