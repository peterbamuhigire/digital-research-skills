# English Output Overlay - Digital Research

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md) | Canonical study: [`docs/continuous-improvement/english-collocations-and-lexical-precision-2026-09-02.md`](../../../docs/continuous-improvement/english-collocations-and-lexical-precision-2026-09-02.md)

Apply this overlay to reports, briefs, source notes, synthesis, and research
correspondence. The standard is polished, literate, exact, and restrained: clear
judgement without pomp, generic filler, or artificial informality.

- Use British English by default, retaining source quotations, proper names, official terms, and the target publication's approved style.
- Separate fact, paraphrase, synthesis, inference, recommendation, assumption, and gap. Language polish never upgrades weak evidence.
- Prefer the ordinary accurate word. Check synonym sense, collocation, grammatical frame, connotation, register, and audience before choosing an advanced word.
- Use intensifiers only when the evidence earns them; otherwise state the number, comparison, mechanism, boundary, or uncertainty.
- Avoid idioms, slogans, stock connectors, vague adjectives, repeated paragraph shapes, false balance, and fashionable AI phrasing.
- Proof names, dates, figures, quotations, citations, links, terminology, spelling, and claim-source alignment. Read aloud where practical.

Record reader, purpose, locale/register, source status, terms checked, inference
labels, language/proof status, unresolved gaps, reviewer, and date. The supplied
books are durable language concepts, not current research, legal, policy, technology,
or standards authority.

## Revision order (five passes)

1. Name the reader, situation, purpose, and next action.
2. Choose the genre and write descriptive headings that carry the argument.
3. Separate fact, interpretation, recommendation, assumption, and gap; put proof beside each claim.
4. Revise paragraphs, then sentences, then words: one centre of gravity per paragraph (point, support, implication), parallel lists, transitions only where a real relationship exists.
5. Proof mechanically: names, dates, figures, links, punctuation, terminology; read aloud and skim the headings alone.

Keep substantive revision (passes 1-4) apart from proof (pass 5). Do not polish a paragraph that should be cut. Establish meaning, actor, time, and relationship before hunting grammar rules.

## Register by output

| Output | Voice required | Typical failure |
|---|---|---|
| Research report | Sourced, exact, candid about inference and limits | Citation laundering, false balance, generic "key findings" |
| Essay (academic or popular) | Arguable thesis, evidence-led, fair countercase | Summary with no point of view |
| Memo or brief | Decision first, only decision-useful context | Buried recommendation, bureaucrat voice |
| Web or blog copy | Specific, scannable, source-aware | Keyword stuffing, universal claims |
| Proposal or plan | Reader's stakes, proof, mechanism, risk, next action | Polish over weak evidence |

## Genericity test

If a passage could fit ten unrelated organisations, add a named actor, place, decision, constraint, example, or source, or cut it. Human quality comes from attention, evidence, and judgement; never imitate people by adding slang, invented anecdotes, or deliberate errors.
