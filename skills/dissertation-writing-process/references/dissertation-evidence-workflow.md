# Dissertation Evidence Workflow

This reference is a self-contained operational synthesis prepared from Megan
Krone, *Navigating the Dissertation Writing Process*. It does not reproduce the
source; it converts its chapter-level workflow into evidence and review gates.

## Working registers

Maintain four small registers:

| Register | Minimum fields |
|---|---|
| Literature | source ID, question, method, finding, limitation, relevance, citation |
| Chapter map | research question, section, evidence, analysis, claim, uncertainty |
| Writing queue | section, next action, dependency, owner, review date |
| Submission | institution rule, check, evidence, reviewer, status |

## Review loop

At each chapter boundary, check: purpose, question coverage, evidence quality,
argument order, method fit, limitation honesty, citation accuracy, and handoff to
the next chapter. A failed check returns the manuscript to the smallest affected
register rather than triggering a wholesale rewrite.

## Responsible AI boundary

AI may assist with planning, explanation, language editing, or structured
feedback when the institution permits it. The researcher remains responsible for
the research question, source selection, interpretation, analysis, citations,
participant protection, and final wording. Record material use and verify every
claim or reference introduced by the tool.
