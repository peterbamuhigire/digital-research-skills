## Pre-Mortem

For the preferred option, state:

- Failure story in one paragraph.
- Leading indicators that the failure story is emerging.
- Mitigations or staged tests.
- Kill criteria.
- Review date.

## Decision Ship Gate

- [ ] Decision owner and deadline visible.
- [ ] Criteria and disqualifiers stated before option ranking.
- [ ] Options include live alternatives, not straw options.
- [ ] Evidence quality differs by option where the corpus differs.
- [ ] Uncertainty is explicit and calibrated.
- [ ] Implementation owner and next action are named.
- [ ] Dissenting view or strongest objection is included.
