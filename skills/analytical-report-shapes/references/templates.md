# Templates

## Decision Memo

1. Decision needed
2. Recommendation
3. Options considered
4. Evidence and tradeoffs
5. Risks, uncertainty, and dissent
6. Next action, owner, deadline

## Current Brief

1. BLUF
2. What changed
3. Why it matters
4. Evidence
5. Confidence and gaps
6. Watch points

## Warning Note

1. Warning judgment
2. Indicators observed
3. Probability and confidence
4. Alternative explanations
5. What would confirm or disconfirm
6. Recommended monitoring or action

## Estimative Memo

1. Key judgments
2. Scope and time horizon
3. Hypotheses considered
4. Evidence by hypothesis
5. Probability and confidence
6. Dissent and indicators

## Investigation Report

1. Question or allegation
2. Method and source limits
3. Findings
4. Evidence trail
5. Contradictions and gaps
6. Conclusion and next investigative steps

## Shape Ship Gate

- [ ] Reader action is explicit.
- [ ] Shape matches mode and lifespan.
- [ ] First page or screen carries the governing message.
- [ ] Evidence trail is adequate for stakes.
- [ ] Uncertainty is visible, not hidden.
- [ ] Next action is named when the shape is decision-facing.
