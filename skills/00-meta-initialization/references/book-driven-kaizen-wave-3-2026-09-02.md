# Book-driven Kaizen Wave 3: digital-research application

Use this reference when improving research design, retrieval, source evaluation, OSINT,
benchmarking, datasets, evidence packs, or research agents.

Owning skill surface: [Kaizen audit reference](kaizen-engine-and-product-audit.md).

## Transfer controls

- Treat retrieval as an evidence pipeline: define need and access boundary, collect provenance,
  index/retrieve with transparent evaluation, verify claims, expose contradictions, and retain
  reproducible query/context records.
- Choose exploration versus exploitation deliberately for research waves and benchmarks. Define
  stopping rules, uncertainty, selection bias, drift signals, and guardrails before adaptive
  collection or ranking changes.
- Combine static inspection, dynamic observation, and safe fuzzing concepts defensively; never
  turn a historical bug case into an unauthorised exploit recipe.
- Delegate research agents with least privilege, explicit source/quality/safety/switch controls,
  human gates for consequential outputs, and a decision log.

## Boundaries

The IR, bandit, OS, HTTP, AI, and SEO books are conceptual or historical inputs. They do not
override current source tiers, access rules, privacy, platform policy, or security guidance.
Protect retrieval authorization boundaries, label inference, and mark incomplete sources or
unverified claims `NOT_ASSESSED`.

## Measures and routing

Track load-bearing claims with verified IDs, numeric spot-check rate, unresolved claims,
contradiction handling, retrieval precision/recall or task measure, reproducibility, reviewer
agreement, collection cost, drift, and time to standardise learning.

## Currentness gate

For every volatile claim record authoritative source, access date, freshness, support,
uncertainty, and review trigger. Recheck current standards, APIs, policies, and technologies
at task time; the empty prompt-engineering source remains `NOT_ASSESSED`.
